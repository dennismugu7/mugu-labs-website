Every stylist knows the feeling. The chair is ready, the products are out, the 11:30 slot was turned down for someone else, and the client doesn't come. No call, no message. Two hours of your day just went quiet.

A small deposit fixes most of this. But many salon owners hesitate, and the reason is rarely money. It's the worry that asking will feel cold, or push loyal clients away.

## Why deposits work

A deposit turns a booking from a casual "maybe" into a small commitment. Clients who've paid something are far more likely to turn up, or to tell you in time when they can't. Even when someone does cancel, you're not left with nothing for a slot you held.

And with M-Pesa, paying a deposit takes seconds. The friction is low for the client and the protection is real for you.

## How to ask without it feeling cold

**Explain the why, in your own voice.**
"We ask for a small deposit to hold your slot, because when someone doesn't show, a stylist loses that time and another client misses out." People respect honesty more than policy language.

**Keep it small and proportionate.**
The deposit should protect your time, not feel like a penalty. Many salons tie it to the length or price of the service: a bigger share for a four-hour braids appointment, little or none for a quick trim.

**Make it count towards the bill.**
Say clearly that the deposit comes off the total on the day. It's not an extra charge, it's an early part of the payment.

**Say what happens if plans change.**
Decide your rule before you need it, then write it down. For example: cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours ahead and the deposit moves to your new booking; no-shows forfeit it.

**Leave room for kindness.**
Emergencies happen. A rule gives you something fair to fall back on, but you can always choose to refund a regular who had a genuinely rough day. Being the one who decides is the point.

## A message you can copy

> Hi! To confirm your booking for **[service]** on **[day, time]**, please send a deposit of **KES [amount]** to **[till/number]**. It comes off your total on the day. If you need to change your time, just let us know 24 hours ahead and we'll move it. See you soon!

Adjust it until it sounds like you.
