## Part C — M2: verify the build against the comps

This is the milestone the whole design depends on. Nobody has yet compared what
the browser renders to what was drawn, at the same scale, side by side. Until
that exists we are all guessing.

Work in `docs/site/review/M2/`.

### C1. Goldens

Playwright against the built export (`npm run start`, or `npx serve out`) — not
the dev server, because we want what actually ships.

Two viewports:

| Name | Viewport | Scale | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `desktop` | 1920 × 1080 | 1 | Same pixel dimensions as the comps, so a sheet is 1:1 with no resampling |
| `mobile` | 390 × 844 | 2 | The phone the owner will check it on |

**Freeze motion before every capture.** Inject
`*, *::before, *::after { animation: none !important; }` into the page. Three
loops run forever — `float` on the product icons and post art, `breathe` on the
footer mark, `rail` on the scroll hint — and they will make every golden differ
from the last one. The reveal effects are CSS *transitions*, not animations, so
this leaves them intact. Goldens have to be byte-stable to double as regression
tests.

**Warm the reveals first.** Elements start at `opacity: 0` until an
IntersectionObserver fires. Scroll the whole page in half-viewport steps with a
short pause at each, then return to your target and wait ~1s before capturing.
Skipping this gives you blank sections and a false alarm.

**The two pinned statements need a specific scroll position.** They are
`210vh` tall with a sticky child; at the top or bottom of that range the text is
mid-blur by design. Capture the hold frame:

```
y = sectionTop + (section.offsetHeight - window.innerHeight) * 0.5
```

then wait ~1s.

Capture one golden per comp folder, named after it:

```
docs/site/review/M2/shots/desktop__01-hero.png
docs/site/review/M2/shots/mobile__01-hero.png
…through 10-contact-and-footer
```

Comps 2 and 3 are one section in the build (DECISIONS D3) and comp 6 is the
heading above comp 7's cards (D4) — capture the built section once and note in
the index which comps it answers. Do not fake a split to match the slides.

Also capture, outside the comp set:

- `desktop__products-detail.png` — `/products/bookflow/`, full page
- `desktop__contact-menu.png` — the contact menu open
- `desktop__reduced-motion.png` — full page with

### C4. Check these yourself before handing over

You will catch most of it faster than I will:

- **Scale.** Is the hero headline the same proportion of the viewport as in
  comp 1? Wrong render scale makes everything look subtly oversized and is the
  single most common failure here.
- **Type.** Sizes, weights, letter-spacing, and whether any heading wraps to a
  different number of lines than the comp.
- **Glyphs.** Every social icon, the arrows, the GitHub mark — an empty box
  means a missing mapping, not a font problem.
- **Gradient.** Banding on the large fills; and whether the blue→violet
  transition around the "How I work" section has a visible hard edge.
- **Glass.** Cards should have a blurred translucent fill. If `backdrop-filter`
  silently does nothing, they will look flat.
- **The 3D art** in the journal cards: breaking above the card top as in comp 7,
  not clipped, not overlapping the titles.
- **Mobile.** No horizontal scroll at 390px. Gutters not tighter than 16px. Nav
  collapsed. Socials in 3 × 2, not 5 + 1.
- **Console.** Zero errors, zero failed requests, on every page.
